⚠️ Internship Safety Tips

  • Never pay money to receive an internship offer.
  • Verify the company and recruiter before sharing personal information.
  • Do not share passwords, OTPs, or banking details.
  • Read the internship description carefully before applying.
  • Use official company websites whenever possible.
